About the author

She believes the most interesting stories aren't always about what people do, but what they're willing to hide.

Courtney Hamm is an independent mystery and thriller author based in Pennsylvania. With a love for dark mysteries, complicated characters, and unexpected twists, she creates stories that explore the secrets people keep, the lies they tell, and the lengths they'll go to protect them.

Outside of writing, Courtney is a tattoo artist and the owner of Black Rabbit Tattoo. Her passion for creativity extends beyond the page — whether she's bringing artwork to life on skin or creating fictional worlds filled with suspense, mystery, and unforgettable characters. When she isn't writing or tattooing, she enjoys spending time with her family and pets, usually while another story idea is already taking shape.
